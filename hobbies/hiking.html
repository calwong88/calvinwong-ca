Hiking & the Outdoors

{% include picture.html src="hiking-wide" alt="Calvin standing on a hiking trail outdoors" %}

Getting outdoors and onto a trail is one of my favourite ways to reset. Hiking gives me the same kind of focus I bring to detail-heavy work — steady pace, clear head, and paying attention to what's around me. It's also one of the few times I'm genuinely off my phone for hours at a stretch, which says a lot these days.
